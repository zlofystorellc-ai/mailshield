🛡️ Welcome to MailShield

MailShield checks the emails you open in Gmail for phishing, UPI/bank fraud, OTP scams and dangerous links or attachments.

Always on, fully local

Basic checks (fake links, look-alike bank domains, risky attachments, scam wording in English and Hinglish) run inside your browser. Nothing leaves your computer for these.

Optional: cloud protection

With your permission, MailShield also checks links against Google Safe Browsing, VirusTotal and URLhaus, and warns you about scams other users have reported. For each email you open, it sends:

  • the sender's address and display name, and the subject line (stored only as a hash);
  • the links in the email and attachment file names (never the files);
  • a short, normalized excerpt of the text with amounts, numbers, dates, names, phone numbers, emails, UPI IDs and account numbers removed;
  • the local risk score and reasons.

MailShield does not store the body of the emails you read. Scan records (domains, hashes, score) are deleted automatically after 90 days.

When you report a scam

Only if you press Report as scam and tick the consent box, the email's text is sent after personal details are removed on your computer (account/card numbers, phone numbers, emails, UPI IDs, OTP codes, PAN/Aadhaar, and your own name/email).

Your control

You get an anonymous install ID — no Google account access, no sign-in. You can switch cloud protection off or delete all your data at any time from the MailShield popup.
